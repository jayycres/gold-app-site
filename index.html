Nutrition

Know exactly where your day stands.

A clear daily picture of calories, protein, carbs and fat, so every meal is a decision, not a guess.

  • Log by barcode, photo or search
  • Daily goals and weekly progress
  • Smart food ratings and suggestions
Competition prep

Built for show day.

For competitors, Competition Mode plans backward from the show: a countdown, prep macros and the pace to hit stage weight.

  • Show-day countdown and prep phases
  • Weekly pace and macro check-ins
  • Peak week and posing practice
Training

Every workout, ready when you are.

Pick a muscle group and go. Routines refresh on a schedule so training never gets stale.

  • 14 workout categories, from Chest to Home
  • Check off sets with a built-in timer
  • PR tracking and a plate calculator
Community

Your gym's own feed.

Posts, stories, announcements and feedback, visible only to your gym. Moderated by your team.

  • Photos, videos and stories
  • Gym announcements and member feedback
  • Leaderboards and trophies
Group training

Post a class. Fill it up.

Trainers post classes with the time, place and spots. Members sign up in one tap and see who else is going.

For trainers

Coach every client from one place.

See who's on track and who's slipping, send plans and goals, and keep the conversation going between sessions.

  • Client dashboard at a glance
  • Workout plans and macro targets
  • Chat, voice notes and form checks
